What this app is

Overhead is a free tool that estimates satellite visibility using public orbital data (CelesTrak), sun and moon position calculations, and third-party weather data (Open-Meteo). It is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.

Accuracy

Positions are computed using standard orbital propagation methods, but several figures shown in the app are estimates, not guarantees, including:

  • Naked-eye visibility confidence scores and estimated magnitude, which are heuristics, not measured brightness values.
  • AR mode marker placement, which assumes a fixed camera field of view and approximate device orientation.
  • Pass-time predictions, which are short-range forward simulations.

Do not rely on this app for any safety-critical, navigational, or professional astronomical purpose.

Acceptable use

Use this app for personal, non-commercial satellite viewing and curiosity. Do not use it to attempt to identify, track, or interfere with the operation of any spacecraft, or in any way that violates the terms of the third-party data providers it relies on.
